# Midpage Legal Research

Midpage connects Claude to a comprehensive database of US federal and state court opinions. Search case law by legal issue or fact pattern, find quotable passages within opinions, and analyze what courts have held — all hyperlinked to real sources for easy verification.

- Search millions of US court opinions (federal and state) by keyword or natural language query.
- Find the exact quotable passage you need within a specific opinion.
- Analyze opinions: ask what the court held, how it reasoned, or what it said about a specific issue.
- Every result is hyperlinked to the source opinion for independent verification.

## Example use cases

1. Find Supreme Court cases about the dormant commerce clause and internet sales tax.
2. What did the court hold in *Carpenter v. United States* about cell phone location data?
3. Find the passage in *Alice Corp v. CLS Bank* where the Court defines the two-step test.
4. Search for recent circuit court opinions on Section 230 immunity for AI-generated content.
5. Analyze *Dobbs v. Jackson* — what standard did the majority apply to the Mississippi law?

## When to Use

Any research question that requires finding, reading, or analyzing US court opinions. Examples include:

- Researching how courts have ruled on a specific legal issue or doctrine
- Finding cases with similar facts to support or distinguish an argument
- Locating the exact holding, reasoning, or quotable language in a known opinion
- Building a case law section for a brief, memo, or motion
- Verifying that a cited case actually says what someone claims it says

## When Not to Use

- Statutory or regulatory research (Midpage covers court opinions, not legislation)
- Citator analysis / Shepardizing (checking if a case is still good law)
- Non-US jurisdictions (coverage is US federal and state courts)
- Drafting documents (this is a research tool)

## Setup

### Claude Web

1. Open and sign in to [Claude](https://claude.ai).
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
3. Search for **Midpage Legal Research**, connect, and authenticate with your Midpage account.
4. Make sure the Midpage connector is toggled on when chatting.

### Claude Desktop

1. Open Claude Desktop and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
3. Search for **Midpage Legal Research**, connect, and authenticate.
4. Once connected, Midpage tools will appear in your conversations.
