# Harvey AI

Harvey AI brings legal Q&A, Vault document analysis, and specialized research knowledge sources into Claude for Harvey subscribers. Ask general legal questions backed by Harvey's broad legal knowledge, query documents in your Vault projects with source citations, and search specialized research databases — all from within Claude.

- Use Harvey AI to ask general legal questions and receive detailed, sourced answers.
- Analyze documents in Harvey Vault projects with source citations and document references.
- Search specialized research knowledge sources (e.g., UK tax law, jurisdiction-specific databases).
- Discover available knowledge sources and Vault projects.

## Example use cases

1. What are the elements of a breach of contract claim under New York law?
2. What Vault projects do I have access to?
3. Summarize the indemnification clauses across all documents in this Vault project.
4. What is the HMRC guidance on capital gains for non-residents? (using a UK tax knowledge source)
5. What research databases can I search?

## When to Use

Any general legal question answerable from Harvey's broad legal knowledge, or questions that can be answered by searching specialized knowledge sources or analyzing documents in your Vault projects. Examples include:

- The elements or defenses of a claim, or the governing standard for an issue
- How a statute, regulation, or doctrine is being interpreted and applied
- Summarizing, comparing, or extracting key terms from Vault documents
- Querying jurisdiction-specific or practice-area research databases

## When Not to Use

- Drafting or editing documents (use Harvey Assistant or the relevant claude-for-legal plugin skill)
- Uploading or managing Vault files (use Harvey's web interface or the Vault API)
- Analytics or outcome predictions
- Non-legal questions

## Setup

### Claude Web

1. Open and sign in to [Claude](https://claude.ai).
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
3. Search for **Harvey**, connect, and authenticate with your Harvey account.
4. Make sure the Harvey connector is toggled on when chatting.

### Claude Desktop

1. Open Claude Desktop and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
3. Search for **Harvey**, connect, and authenticate with your Harvey account.
4. Once connected, Harvey tools will appear in your conversations.
